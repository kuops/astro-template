This is a personal blog built with [Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/), aiming for a clean, fast and accessible reading experience.

![Site preview](/astropaper-og.jpg)

## About This Site

This is where I keep notes, findings and tutorials from my day-to-day work on development and design. The content focuses on web development — frameworks, tooling and engineering practice. Books are chapter-based guides, while posts are shorter, standalone articles.

## Features

- SEO-friendly
- Fast performance
- Light & dark mode
- Bilingual content with graceful fallback
- Highly customizable
- Organizable books & posts
- Responsive & accessible
- Static search with [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app/)
- Automatic social image generation
